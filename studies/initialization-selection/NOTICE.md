# Provenance

gradient and batch-of-two AGOP order derive from
https://github.com/marceltomas/breaking-data-symmetries at commit
`311273bfc08adcde344c8b021fc5aa8d9970ad98`, under GPL-3.0. The included LICENSE
applies to this derived implementation. New experiment design and analysis were
prepared for Xiao Tian with Codex assistance. This local pilot is not a submitted
or peer-reviewed paper.
